== Overview

ACME Financial Services runs customer-facing workloads on OpenShift. A small platform team keeps that cluster healthy, helps application teams ship, and takes a turn on the incident queue.

You are Sarah, a senior platform engineer at ACME. Morgan, from infosec, is the person who pages you when something looks wrong.

== Your mission

Confirm you can open this cluster and see the two namespaces this workshop uses.

=== Prerequisites

* The roadshow home page setup script has been run (`setup/lab-environment.sh`)
* Console URLs and passwords from the xref:ROOT:index.adoc[home page]

.Sign in to the OpenShift console at {openshift_cluster_console_url} as {openshift_cluster_admin_username} with the password on the home page
[%collapsible]
====
. Open the *OpenShift Console* tab, or go to `{openshift_cluster_console_url}`.
. Sign in with `{openshift_cluster_admin_username}` and the password on the home page.

image::fsi-security/acme-cluster.png[OpenShift console,link=self, window=blank, width=100%]
====

.Run oc whoami, then list pods in payment-gateway-{workshop_user_suffix} and vulnerable-workload-{workshop_user_suffix}. Expect running pods in both. If a namespace is missing, the instructor runs ./setup/deploy-incident-env.sh
[%collapsible]
====
[source,bash,subs="+macros,+attributes",role=execute]
----
oc whoami
oc get pods -n payment-gateway-{workshop_user_suffix}
oc get pods -n vulnerable-workload-{workshop_user_suffix}
----
====

.Sign in to RHACS at {acs_route} as {acs_portal_username} with the password on the home page
[%collapsible]
====
. Open the *Red Hat Advanced Cluster Security* tab, or go to `{acs_route}[{acs_route}^]`.
. Sign in with `{acs_portal_username}` and the password on the home page.
====
